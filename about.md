# GD Streaks

Keep a daily login streak in Geometry Dash, just like the daily streaks you know from TikTok and other apps.

## How it works

The first time you launch the game on a new day counts as your activity for that day. Launch it again the same day and nothing changes. Come back the next day and your streak grows by one. Skip a whole day and the streak restarts at 1, while your best streak is kept forever.

Everything is stored locally in the mod's save data. No account, no internet connection, no external servers.

## In the main menu

A small flame pill sits in the top right corner of the main menu with your current streak next to it. Click it to open the streak window.

## Streak window

- Current streak, shown big next to the flame
- Best streak
- Start date of the current streak
- Date of your last activity
- A 7 day calendar with the days you were active, and a golden ring around today
- Progress towards the next milestone

## Milestones and notifications

A notification appears when your streak grows or resets. The days 7, 14, 30, 50 and 100 have their own messages, and after 100 the next milestone keeps going up in steps of 100.

## Settings

- **Show streak counter** - hide the number next to the flame
- **Hide streak button** - hide the whole flame pill, the streak still counts
- **Streak notifications** - turn the notifications off

## Timezones

The streak follows your local calendar date, so daylight saving changes do not break it. Travelling across timezones never resets or duplicates a day: the local date moving backwards is ignored, and a jump of two local days within one real day is treated as travel rather than a missed day.
